Atelier Legacy

From the Princess Cut to the Next Generation of Diamond Designs

Betzalel Ambar is credited as one of the creators of the modern princess cut diamond — a cut that became part of the global language of fine jewelry. Today, that same spirit of invention continues through original diamond designs, sculptural settings, and one-of-one pieces made with precision and restraint. Bez Ambar jewelry is not assembled from fashion trends. It is drawn, engineered, and refined from the stone outward.

The Elysian Cut™

An Old Idea, Brought to Life

The Elysian Cut™ is not a new shape. It is an old idea — the step cut, centuries in the making — reborn in the oval and the pear. The art is in the angles: the relationship between crown and pavilion is a game of angles, and when those angles are right, the cut comes alive. The skill is in selecting the rough that can support them.

The goal is never a single stone. The goal is a line of brilliance — calibrated stone to stone, until light travels as one continuous line around the finger, the wrist, the neck.
